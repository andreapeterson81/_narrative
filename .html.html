Chunk 1 of 3

The Power of Sensory & Descriptive Language

Telling (Weak)

Tells the reader facts directly. Uses summary statements and simple adjectives. Leaves no room for imagination.

Example: "He was very nervous."

Showing (Strong)

Uses sensory details (sight, sound, touch, smell, taste) and physical actions to paint a picture.

Example: "Sweat dripped down his collar as he tapped his foot violently against the floor."

Chunk 2 of 3

Crafting & Formatting Realistic Dialogue

Rule 1: Quotation Marks

Enclose exact words in quotation marks. Place commas and periods inside the quotation marks.

Rule 2: New Speaker, New Paragraph

Every single time a different person starts speaking, indent and start a brand new paragraph.

Rule 3: Speaker Tags + Action

Combine speech with character action tags instead of relying solely on "he said / she said".
